# Merge products

Two catalog rows for the same thing happen when a bill creates a product you already keep under another name. Merge folds the extra row into the one you want to keep.

Open the product that should disappear and choose **Merge** (next to Delete). Pick the product to keep. A summary lists the history rows and aliases that will move, that this product will be removed, and that its name stays as an alias. Confirm there. The current product is then removed. Its purchases and aliases move over. Its catalog name becomes a global alias on the product you kept, so the next bill with that till wording still matches; see [Product aliases](../product-aliases/index.md) and [Receipt matching](../receipt-matching/index.md).

The two products must use the same unit. Quantities are not converted. The photo on the product you keep stays; if it has none, the other product’s photo is used.
